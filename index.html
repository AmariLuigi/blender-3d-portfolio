About Luigi Amari

General 3D Craft • Materials, Motion & Environment

I am a multidisciplinary 3D artist and motion designer with broad generalist expertise across the computer graphics pipeline. Working in Blender with Cycles and EEVEE Next, my practice explores tactile procedural shaders, hypnotic geometric loops, complex fluid simulations, audio-reactive 3D visualizers, and immersive technology backgrounds.

From the microscopic surface tension of liquid mercury chrome and organic gold filigree to music-synchronized FFT waveform displacements and turbulent ocean dynamics, each project is developed with custom multi-node Bezier paths, physically accurate Fresnel falloffs, and zero-grain denoiser passes.
