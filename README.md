## ✨ Technical Highlights

- **Dual-Stream Engine**: Instant 1080p web-streaming with `+faststart` H.264 plus switchable 4K UHD master renders in-player.
- **Cinema Theater Mode**: Fullscreen custom player with frame-scrub timeline, loop, volume, quality toggle, and artist technical dossier.
- **Color Pipeline**: Graded with AgX high dynamic range transform for pristine specular highlights without clipping.
- **Zero-Grain Raytracing**: Prefiltered OptiX AI denoising across RGB, Albedo, and Normal passes.
